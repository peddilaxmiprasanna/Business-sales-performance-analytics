# Excel Data Analytics Tasks

A collection of Excel-based data analytics tasks focused on business sales performance and customer churn analysis. These tasks demonstrate data cleaning, data analysis, KPI development, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and dashboard design to identify trends and support data-driven decisions.

---

# Task 1: Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Overview

This task focuses on analyzing business sales data to understand revenue trends, product performance, category-wise profitability, and regional sales performance. An Excel dashboard presents key performance indicators (KPIs) and visualizations to identify business trends and opportunities for improvement.

## Key Features

- **Data Cleaning:** Checked and prepared raw sales data for accurate analysis.
- **Sales Analysis:** Examines overall sales performance and revenue trends.
- **Profit Analysis:** Evaluates profitability across products, categories, and regions.
- **Product Performance:** Identifies top-performing products based on sales.
- **Category Analysis:** Compares sales and profit across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.
- **Regional Analysis:** Examines sales and profitability across different regions.
- **Dashboard Visualization:** Presents key metrics and five charts in a clear, organized Excel dashboard.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Data Cleaning and Data Transformation
- Sales and Profit Analysis
- Data Visualization and Dashboard Design

## Objective

To analyze business sales data, identify profitable categories and regions, understand sales trends, and provide insights that support better business decisions.

## Outcome

Developed an Excel dashboard featuring key performance indicators and five visualizations to summarize sales performance, compare profitability, identify trends, and highlight potential business growth opportunities.

# Task 2: Customer Churn Analysis

## Overview

This task focuses on analyzing customer churn patterns and understanding the factors that influence customer retention. It uses customer data to identify churn trends across internet service types, payment methods, contract types, and customer tenure.

An Excel dashboard presents key performance indicators (KPIs) and visualizations to make the findings easier to understand.

## Key Features

- **Data Cleaning:** Checked and prepared customer data for analysis.
- **KPI Summary:** Displays total customers, churn rate, churned customers, and retained customers.
- **Internet Service Analysis:** Examines churn across internet service categories.
- **Payment Method Analysis:** Compares churn patterns across payment methods.
- **Contract Type Analysis:** Explores the relationship between contract types and customer churn.
- **Tenure Analysis:** Examines churn patterns based on customer tenure.
- **Dashboard Visualization:** Presents key metrics and four charts in a clear, organized Excel dashboard.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Data Cleaning and Data Analysis
- KPI Analysis
- Data Visualization and Dashboard Design

## Objective

To identify customer churn patterns, understand potential retention challenges, and support data-driven decisions that can improve customer retention.

## Outcome

Developed a consolidated Excel dashboard to summarize customer churn metrics and visualize patterns across important customer segments, supporting a better understanding of customer retention.

- Exploratory Data Analysis
- KPI Calculation and Reporting

- Dashboard Design and Visualization
- Business Insights and Data-Driven Decision-Making
